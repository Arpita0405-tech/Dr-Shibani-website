Maternity Care

Pregnancy & High-Risk Obstetrics Care

Complete, compassionate antenatal care tailored to ensure a safe journey from conception to delivery. Specialized medical care for high-risk maternal factors.

Key Symptoms & Indicators to Consult:

High Blood Pressure / BP Gestational Diabetes Severe Nausea & Weakness Previous C-Section / Twin Risk
  • Antenatal consultations & regular fetal growth monitoring
  • Pre-conception counseling & tailored pregnancy diet advice

Hormonal Care

PCOS & Menstrual Disorders Management

Comprehensive diagnosis and holistic treatment plans for irregular periods, Polycystic Ovarian Syndrome (PCOS/PCOD), and hormonal imbalance.

Key Symptoms & Indicators to Consult:

Irregular / Delayed Periods Unexplained Weight Gain Severe Acne & Facial Hair Excessive Heavy Bleeding
  • Customized hormonal regulation & fertility guidance
  • Adolescent gynaecology and lifestyle therapy

Keyhole Surgery

Total Laparoscopic Hysterectomy (TLH)

Advanced minimally invasive keyhole surgery to remove the uterus with precision, minimal blood loss, negligible scars, and rapid recovery.

Key Symptoms & Indicators to Consult:

Large Uterine Fibroids Severe Adenomyosis Pain Uncontrolled Heavy Bleeding Uterine Prolapse
  • 1-2 days hospital stay with fast return to routine life
  • Painless postoperative recovery with tiny keyhole marks
Schedule Laparoscopic Advice
Uterus Sparing

Laparoscopic Myomectomy (Fibroid Removal)

Uterus-preserving keyhole surgery designed for women who wish to retain fertility. Only the fibroids are precisely removed while keeping the uterus safe.

Key Symptoms & Indicators to Consult:

Pelvic Pain & Heaviness Frequent Urination Pressure Difficulty Conceiving Pain During Intimacy
  • Fertility-preserving surgery for young women
  • Precision removal of single or multiple fibroids
Book Fibroid Consultation
Minimal Access

Laparoscopic Ovarian Cystectomy

Precision removal of complex ovarian cysts (Dermoid, Endometriomas / Chocolate cysts) while saving healthy ovarian tissue and egg reserve.

Key Symptoms & Indicators to Consult:

Sharp Lower Abdominal Pain Bloating & Swelling Chocolate Cysts / Endometriomas Irregular Menstrual Pain
  • Ovary-sparing laparoscopic cystectomy
  • Protection of future fertility & hormonal health
Consult for Ovarian Cysts
Pelvic Pain Care

Advanced Endometriosis Management

Specialized care for chronic pelvic pain, endometriosis implants, and fertility enhancement through medical and surgical therapies.

Key Symptoms & Indicators to Consult:

Severe Menstrual Cramps Chronic Lower Back Pain Painful Bowel Movements Infertility / Conception Delay
  • Targeted hormonal medical suppression
  • Laparoscopic excision of endometriotic tissue & adhesions
Book Endometriosis Care
Day Care Procedure

Diagnostic & Operative Hysteroscopy

Scarless endo-uterine evaluation using micro-cameras through natural passages to treat polyps, intrauterine septum, or adhesions.

Key Symptoms & Indicators to Consult:

Recurrent Miscarriages Uterine Polyps / Septum Post-Menopausal Bleeding Abnormal Spotting
  • Zero external cuts or skin incisions
  • Same-day discharge day-care procedure
