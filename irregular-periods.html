Women's Health

Understanding Irregular Periods

Irregular periods are a common concern among women of all ages. They may occur due to hormonal imbalance, stress, PCOS, thyroid disorders, excessive exercise, or lifestyle changes.

Common Symptoms

  • ✔ Missed periods
  • ✔ Very heavy or very light bleeding
  • ✔ Cycles shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days
  • ✔ Severe menstrual pain
  • ✔ Spotting between periods

Possible Causes

Conditions such as PCOS, thyroid problems, stress, sudden weight changes, pregnancy, or certain medications can affect the menstrual cycle.

When to See a Doctor

If your periods remain irregular for several months, are extremely painful, or involve excessive bleeding, consult a gynecologist for proper evaluation and treatment.

Treatment

Treatment depends on the underlying cause and may include lifestyle changes, medication, or hormonal therapy as advised by your doctor.
