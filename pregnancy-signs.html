Pregnancy Care

Early Signs of Pregnancy

Every pregnancy is unique, but many women notice some common early symptoms. Recognizing these signs early helps you seek timely medical care and begin a healthy pregnancy journey.

Common Symptoms

  • ✔ Missed menstrual period
  • ✔ Nausea or morning sickness
  • ✔ Breast tenderness
  • ✔ Fatigue and tiredness
  • ✔ Frequent urination
  • ✔ Mild cramping or spotting

When Should You Take a Pregnancy Test?

A home pregnancy test is most accurate after a missed period. If the result is positive, schedule an appointment with your gynecologist to confirm the pregnancy and begin prenatal care.

Why Early Prenatal Care is Important

Regular checkups, healthy nutrition, folic acid supplements, and lifestyle guidance help ensure the health of both the mother and the baby throughout pregnancy.
