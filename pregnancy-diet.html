Pregnancy Nutrition

Healthy Pregnancy Diet

A balanced diet during pregnancy is essential for the health of both the mother and the growing baby. Eating nutritious foods supports healthy development and reduces the risk of complications.

Foods to Include

  • ✔ Fresh fruits and vegetables
  • ✔ Whole grains
  • ✔ Milk, curd and other dairy products
  • ✔ Eggs, fish, lean meat and pulses
  • ✔ Dry fruits and healthy nuts

Important Nutrients

Folic acid, iron, calcium, protein and Vitamin D are important nutrients during pregnancy. Take supplements only as advised by your doctor.

Foods to Avoid

Avoid alcohol, smoking, excessive caffeine, raw or undercooked meat, and unhygienic food. Always choose fresh and well-cooked meals.

Stay Hydrated

Drink plenty of water throughout the day to stay hydrated and support your baby's healthy growth.
