Meet Dr. Shibani

MBBS, MS (Obstetrics & Gynecology) | Advanced Laparoscopic Surgeon

Dr. Shibani is a leading Obstetrician, Gynecologist, and Laparoscopic Surgeon based in Bhubaneswar. Over her 10+ years of dedicated clinical practice, she has built a reputation for offering precise diagnostic evaluations, minimally invasive surgical solutions, and compassionate patient care.

Surgical & Academic Mastery

Specializes in minimal access keyhole surgeries (TLH, Laparoscopic Myomectomy, Ovarian Cystectomy) resulting in faster recovery, minimal pain, and minimal surgical marks.

Compassionate Care Philosophy
