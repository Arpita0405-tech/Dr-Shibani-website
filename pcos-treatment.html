PCOS: Symptoms, Causes & Treatment

Everything you need to know about Polycystic Ovary Syndrome, its symptoms, causes, diagnosis and treatment.

Women's Health

What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is one of the most common hormonal disorders affecting women of reproductive age. It can lead to irregular periods, weight gain, acne, excessive hair growth, difficulty in conceiving and hormonal imbalance.

Common Symptoms

  • ✔ Irregular or missed periods
  • ✔ Weight gain
  • ✔ Acne and oily skin
  • ✔ Excess facial or body hair
  • ✔ Hair thinning
  • ✔ Difficulty in getting pregnant

Causes of PCOS

The exact cause of PCOS is not fully understood. Genetics, insulin resistance, hormonal imbalance and lifestyle factors may all contribute to its development.

Treatment

Treatment depends on your symptoms and future pregnancy plans. It may include healthy lifestyle changes, weight management, medications to regulate periods and ovulation, and regular follow-up with a gynecologist.

When Should You Visit a Doctor?

If you have irregular periods, severe acne, unwanted hair growth, or difficulty conceiving, consult a gynecologist for proper evaluation and treatment.
