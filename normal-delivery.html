Pregnancy Care

Normal Delivery vs C-Section

Both Normal Delivery and Cesarean (C-Section) are safe methods of childbirth. The right choice depends on the mother's health, baby's condition, and your doctor's medical advice.

Benefits of Normal Delivery

  • ✔ Faster recovery
  • ✔ Shorter hospital stay
  • ✔ Lower risk of infection
  • ✔ Early breastfeeding
  • ✔ Less postoperative pain

When is a C-Section Recommended?

A Cesarean delivery may be recommended in cases such as breech baby, multiple pregnancy, placenta previa, fetal distress, prolonged labour, or when a normal delivery may not be safe for the mother or baby.

Recovery After Delivery

Whether you have a normal delivery or a C-section, proper rest, healthy nutrition, regular follow-up visits, and following your doctor's advice are essential for a smooth recovery.
